BNOC - Big Name On Campus

It’s your first day at university, make sure you are sat in the best seat in class to become the Big Name On Campus!

Get your player to be positioned in the best spot come the time the lecture room is full. Your score is based on the number of points of those who you are sat within touching distance of. The player with the highest score wins.

Shuffle the deck, hand each player 5 starting cards, and place the main Lecture Hall grid template in the center of the table.

There are two primary card pools: Character Cards (who you sit next to) and Action Cards (how you manipulate the room seating arrangements).

Draw one card from the pile, play one Action Card from your hand, or choose to position a character token onto an empty seat grid square.

Your primary BNOC token must be hidden until mid-game, or deployed strategically when your favorite archetype cards surround an open hot-seat.

Calculate the combined social status integers of all touching adjacent squares (horizontal, vertical, and diagonal) directly surrounding your marker.

The game reaches its immediate conclusion when the final row seat configuration is occupied or the lecturer walks through the door card!
